About

Journalism, editorial work and visual storytelling.

Reece Murry is a student journalist at Coppell High School in Coppell, Texas. Her work includes sports reporting, feature writing, opinion columns and photography for The Sidekick and Coppell Student Media.

She has also contributed to Coppell Athletic Media, gaining experience in live sports production and visual coverage.

This portfolio brings together her published reporting, editorial experience and recognition.
